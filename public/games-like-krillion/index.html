FOR THE UNEXPECTED THINGS YOU KNOW

Games like Krillion.
A different discovery.

If you enjoy Krillion’s open-answer questions and the challenge of finding a less obvious correct answer, try RARE. Five questions become five discoveries in an interactive crystal collection.

Make a discovery ↗

RARE is an independent game. You can play the original Krillion at krillion.io.

One prompt. Plenty of possibilities.

Read a category prompt and type one answer. Your task is to choose something that fits while going beyond the first example that comes to mind. Each correct answer earns points based on its fixed editorial rarity rating.

RARE gives you five prompts per session and thirty seconds for each active prompt. Common answers earn 100 points, uncommon answers 250, rare answers 500 and exceptional answers 1,000. Your answers become quartz, jade, amethyst and amber specimens in a five-piece collection. Incorrect answers and skips create an unformed specimen worth zero.

These are game rewards and editorial difficulty tiers. The question lists, artwork and scoring belong to this independent game.

A daily game, or a little more practice.

Everyone shares the same five prompts for a given UTC date. A new daily discovery begins at midnight UTC. An anonymous browser cookie remembers your attempt so you can resume after refreshing.

Unlimited practice offers fresh sets after your daily game. The full published bank covers science, geography, movies, books, music, games and other subjects. Practice collections are shown after each session, while the leaderboard counts completed daily games.

If you arrived searching for Krillion practice or unlimited play, this site offers its own independent game. For Krillion’s official modes and current daily game, visit the original site linked above.

Finding the less obvious answer.

  1. Read the boundary. A prompt about planets and one about dwarf planets accept different answers.
  2. Recall an example, then look further. Use the first example as a starting point for something less familiar.
  3. Keep it accurate. A common correct answer earns points. An unusual incorrect answer earns zero.
  4. Explore your finished collection. After all five questions, you can inspect the accepted answer lists and their scores.

Take your time between questions.

After you submit, the crystal reveal shows your rarity tier and score. You can rotate the specimen and inspect your collection. The following question stays hidden until you press Keep Discovering. Its thirty-second clock starts when the server reveals it. An active question continues counting down when you leave the page.

A collection without an account.

Play directly in your browser. Your anonymous game results are stored for scoring and the leaderboard, and local storage holds your device’s daily collection history. Sound is optional. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and a simplified illustration is used when 3D graphics are unavailable.

When your collection is complete, share a spoiler-free scorecard to challenge someone else.
