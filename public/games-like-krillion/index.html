What kind of game is it?

You see a category prompt and type one answer. There are several possible correct answers, so the challenge is choosing something that fits while going beyond the first thing that comes to mind. You do not need to guess a hidden single word.

In RARE DIVE, each expedition has five prompts and each active prompt gives you thirty seconds. Correct answers earn 100, 250, 500 or 1,000 points according to fixed editorial rarity tiers. Every point moves your explorer two meters deeper into a pixel ocean, with a maximum depth of 10,000 meters.

Those tiers are authored difficulty ratings. They are not live measurements of how often Krillion players, or RARE DIVE players, choose each answer. Our answer bank and scoring are independent.

Daily trivia or more practice?

The daily dive gives everyone the same five prompts for a given UTC date. Your browser remembers your attempt, so refreshing brings you back to the current game. A new daily dive begins at midnight UTC.

Choose Unlimited from the start screen for fresh practice rounds after your daily game. Practice uses the full published bank of question sets, spanning science, geography, movies, books, games, music and more. Practice scores do not enter the daily leaderboard.

If you came here searching for “Krillion unlimited,” this is a separate practice game. It does not unlock an official Krillion mode, use your Krillion account, or provide the official daily answers.

How to find rarer correct answers

  1. Read the exact boundary. “Planet” and “dwarf planet” are different categories. A rare answer only scores if it fits.
  2. Think of a familiar example, then move sideways. Use what you know about the category to recall a less obvious member.
  3. Choose accuracy over obscurity. A common correct answer beats an unusual incorrect one.
  4. Explore the answer list after finishing. RARE DIVE shows accepted answers and their points after all five rounds, so you can learn for the next expedition.

Does the timer run between prompts?

No. After you submit an answer, the result screen shows your rarity tier, points and earned depth. The following prompt stays hidden until you press Keep Diving. Its clock begins when the server reveals it. Once it is active, closing the tab or opening a menu does not pause it.

Can I play without signing up?

Yes. No email or account is needed. An anonymous browser cookie identifies your daily attempt; device-local storage holds your logbook. The game offers optional sound, a reduced-motion experience and a copyable, spoiler-free dive report to challenge a friend.
